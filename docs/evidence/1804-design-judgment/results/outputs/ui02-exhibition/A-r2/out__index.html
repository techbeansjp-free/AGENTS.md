展覧会について

かつて市の沿岸で盛んだった海苔養殖を、ベか舟(一人乗りの平底舟)、海苔すき道具、漁師の日記、写真約120点でたどります。

English Information for visitors

A Century of Tidal Flats and Nori: Boats and Handwork

This exhibition traces the history of nori (seaweed) farming, once a thriving industry along our city's coast. Through a beka boat (a one-person flat-bottomed boat), nori-making tools, a fisherman's diaries and about 120 photographs, it shows a way of life that ended when the tidal flats were reclaimed.

Dates: Sat, April 10 – Sun, June 27, 2027

Hours: 9:30–17:00 (last entry 16:30). Closed on Mondays (if Monday is a public holiday, closed the next weekday).

Admission: Adults ¥400; high school and university students ¥200; free for junior high school students and younger, visitors 65 and over, and people with a disability certificate plus one companion.

Venue: Municipal Local History Museum, 2F Special Exhibition Room, 3-14 Shiomi-cho, Konan Ward.

Access: 8-minute walk from Shiomi Station (South Exit). Free parking for 12 cars.

Accessibility: Elevator, 2 wheelchairs for loan, accessible restroom on the 1st floor.

Related events (free, 20 people each, reservation required): Apr 24 Gallery talk 14:00 · May 15 Nori-making workshop 10:00 (elementary school age and up) · Jun 5 Talk with a former fisherman 14:00. Reserve by phone 045-000-1234 (9:30–17:00) or via the web form.
