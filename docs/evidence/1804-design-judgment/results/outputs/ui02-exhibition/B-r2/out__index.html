展覧会について

かつて市の沿岸で盛んだった海苔養殖を、ベか舟、海苔すき道具、漁師の日記、写真約120点でたどります。干潟の埋立てで途絶えた暮らしを、道具と記録から伝える展覧会です。

  • ベか舟一人乗りの平底舟。浅い干潟を進むための舟です。
  • 海苔すき道具海苔を一枚ずつ手で仕上げた道具です。
  • 漁師の日記日々の天気や作業を書きとめた記録です。
  • 写真 約120点養殖の現場と暮らしの様子を伝える写真です。

English information

This exhibition traces a century of nori (seaweed) farming along our city’s coast. About 120 photographs, a beka boat (a flat-bottomed, one-person boat), nori-making tools and a fisherman’s diaries show how people worked the tidal flats. It also tells how this way of life ended when the flats were reclaimed.

  • Dates: Sat 10 April – Sun 27 June 2027
  • Hours: 9:30–17:00 (last entry 16:30). Closed Mondays (the next weekday if Monday is a public holiday).
  • Admission: Adults ¥400; high school and university students ¥200; free for junior high school students and younger, visitors 65 and over, and visitors with a disability certificate plus one companion.
  • Venue: Special Exhibition Room, 2F, Municipal Local History Museum, 3-14 Shiomi-cho, Konan Ward. 8 minutes on foot from Shiomi Station (South Exit). Free parking for 12 cars.
  • Accessibility: Elevator, 2 wheelchairs for loan, accessible restroom on the 1st floor.
  • Events: Three free events (20 people each, advance booking required; see the Japanese schedule below). Book by phone at +81-45-000-1234 (9:30–17:00) or via the booking form. Japanese-language events.
