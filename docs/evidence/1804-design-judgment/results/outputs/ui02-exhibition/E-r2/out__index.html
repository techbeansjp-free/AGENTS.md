展覧会について

かつて市の沿岸で盛んだった海苔養殖を、ベか舟（一人乗りの平底舟）、海苔すき道具、漁師の日記、写真約120点でたどります。干潟の埋立てで途絶えた暮らしを、道具と記録から伝える展覧会です。

English Information

A Century of Tidal Flats and Nori: Boats and Handwork

This exhibition traces the history of nori (seaweed) farming, once thriving along our city's coast. Through flat-bottomed one-person boats, nori-making tools, fishermen's diaries and about 120 photographs, it shows a way of life that ended when the tidal flats were reclaimed.
