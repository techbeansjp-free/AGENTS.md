English Information

A Century of Tidal Flats and Nori: Boats and Handwork

This exhibition traces the history of nori (seaweed) farming, once thriving along our city's coast. Through beka boats (single-person flat-bottomed boats), nori-making tools, a fisherman's diaries and about 120 photographs, it tells the story of a way of life that ended when the tidal flats were reclaimed.
