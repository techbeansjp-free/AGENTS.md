English information

A Century of Tidal Flats and Nori: Boats and Handwork
Nori (seaweed) farming once thrived along our city’s coast. This exhibition traces its history through a beka-bune (a one-person flat-bottomed boat), nori-making tools, a fisherman’s diary and about 120 photographs. It shows how a way of life ended when the tidal flats were reclaimed.
