展覧会について

かつて市の沿岸で盛んだった海苔養殖を、ベか舟（一人乗りの平底舟）、海苔すき道具、漁師の日記、写真約120点でたどります。

干潟の埋立てで途絶えた暮らしを、道具と記録から伝える展覧会です。

English Information

A Century of Tidal Flats and Nori: Boats and Handwork

Nori seaweed farming once thrived along our city's coast. Through a one-person flat-bottomed boat (beka-bune), nori-making tools, fishermen's diaries and about 120 photographs, this exhibition traces that way of life. It was lost when the tidal flats were reclaimed.
