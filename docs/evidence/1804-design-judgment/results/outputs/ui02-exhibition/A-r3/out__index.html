展覧会についてAbout the exhibition

かつて市の沿岸で盛んだった海苔養殖を、ベか舟（一人乗りの平底舟）、海苔すき道具、漁師の日記、写真約120点でたどります。干潟の埋立てで途絶えた暮らしを、道具と記録から伝えます。

  • ベか舟一人乗りの平底舟
  • 海苔すき道具手仕事の道具
  • 漁師の日記暮らしの記録
  • 写真 約120点干潟の風景と人々

English Information

A Century of Tidal Flats and Nori: Boats and Handwork

This exhibition traces the history of nori (seaweed) farming, once thriving along our city's coast, through beka boats (flat-bottomed, one-person boats), nori-making tools, fishermen's diaries and about 120 photographs. It shows how a way of life ended when the tidal flats were reclaimed.
