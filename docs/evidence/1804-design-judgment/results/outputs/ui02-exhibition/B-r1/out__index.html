English information

A Century of Tidal Flats and Nori: Boats and Handwork

This exhibition traces the laver (nori) farming that once thrived along our city's coast. It brings together a beka boat (a one-person flat-bottomed boat), nori-making tools, a fisherman's diaries and about 120 photographs. Through these tools and records, it tells the story of a way of life that ended when the tidal flats were reclaimed.
