English Information

A Century of Tidal Flats and Nori: Boats and Handwork

This exhibition traces the history of nori (seaweed) farming, once thriving along the city's coast, through a one-person flat-bottomed boat called a beka-bune, nori-making tools, a fisherman's diaries and about 120 photographs. It tells the story of a way of life that ended when the tidal flats were reclaimed.
