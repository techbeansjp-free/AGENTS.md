English Information

This exhibition traces the history of nori (seaweed) farming, once thriving along the city's coast. It presents a one-person flat-bottomed boat (beka-bune), nori-making tools, fishermen's diaries and about 120 photographs. Through these objects and records, it tells the story of a way of life that ended when the tidal flats were reclaimed.

展覧会について

かつて市の沿岸で盛んだった海苔養殖を、ベか舟（一人乗りの平底舟）、海苔すき道具、漁師の日記、写真約120点でたどります。干潟の埋立てで途絶えた暮らしを、道具と記録から伝えます。
